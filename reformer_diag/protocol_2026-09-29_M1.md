Metric ambiguity is declared before running. Conventional trapezoidal IAE is sum(dt*(abs(e[k])+abs(e[k-1]))/2). The paper's printed Eq.13 instead puts abs outside the average of signed errors. Preserve both raw values and their relative deviations.
