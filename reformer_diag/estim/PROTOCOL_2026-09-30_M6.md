# M6 protocol: health estimator (v0), frozen before any estimator result

Frozen 2026-09-30 ~03:25 IST. Authority: Bosco, chat 2026-09-30 ("can you try finsihe this laso M6
(estimator), M7 (controllers) and M8"). Scope: Mode 1, simulation, quasi-steady plant (validated
by V1, max 0.027% of nominal H2), declared noise model of the M2-M4 protocol. v0 = first version.

## Estimated quantities
Catalyst multiplier a_c and membrane multiplier a_m, each in [0.4, 1]. Hydrogen-meter drift and
feed shortfall are NOT in the estimator's model; histories containing them test whether the
estimator answers "deteriorated, cause not established" instead of blaming the wrong part.

## Estimator model
Bicubic interpolation of the 2-D nominal np=50 map (a_c in {1,.9,...,.4} x a_m in
{1,.95,.9,.85,.8,.7,.6}) at the two feed levels used (0.0021 and 0.0024 mol/min). Outside the
map's a_m range the estimate is clipped at the bound and flagged.

## Measurements (as M2-M4 protocol)
Set S4: permeate H2, outlet T, waste flow (0.1-min samples), micro-GC H2/CH4/CO/CO2 mole
fractions (every 3 min, available 3 min later). Noise sd and bias bounds as the M2-M4 table; one
constant bias per signal per history, drawn uniformly within its bound (seeded). Samples in the
first minute after any feed change are discarded (settling).

## Excitation
PASSIVE: constant 0.0021 mol/min. ACTIVE: +0.0003 mol/min for 6 min every 20 min, first at t = 14 min.

## Estimators
- MHE: every 1 min, weighted least squares over the last 10 min using per-feature window means at
  each feed level present; variance of a mean = sd^2/n + B^2/3 (bias as uniform uncertainty);
  arrival cost from the previous estimate with random-walk variance (0.002/min)^2 per min per
  parameter; bounds [0.4, 1]; covariance from (J^T W J + P0^-1)^-1.
- EKF baseline: random-walk state (a_c, a_m), same measurement model per sample,
  R = sd^2 + B^2/3, Jacobian by central differences on the map; clipped to bounds.

## Classification (every minute)
z_c = (1 - a_c_hat)/sd_c, z_m = (1 - a_m_hat)/sd_m. CATALYST if z_c > 2.58 and z_m <= 2.58;
MEMBRANE if the reverse; BOTH if both > 2.58. If neither, and the window-mean H2 is below nominal
by more than 3 sd_mean + bias bound: NOT_ESTABLISHED; else HEALTHY. Consistency check: if the
MHE fit's normalised residual sum exceeds the chi-square 0.99 quantile for its degrees of
freedom, a CATALYST/MEMBRANE/BOTH call is replaced by NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## Held-out test histories (60 min; defined now; the estimator is not tuned on them)
H1 catalyst exp(-0.01 t) [authors]; H2 membrane exp(-0.006 t) [authors]; H3 catalyst linear
1 -> 0.6; H4 membrane step to 0.9 at t = 20; H5 healthy; H6 H2-meter drift -0.02% of nominal
per min from t = 10; H7 both: catalyst exp(-0.005 t) and membrane exp(-0.003 t).
Correct final call: H1,H3 CATALYST; H2,H4 MEMBRANE; H5 HEALTHY; H6 NOT_ESTABLISHED or HEALTHY;
H7 BOTH.

## Truth models (plant used to generate the data)
nominal (same model as the estimator); kinpert (the four kinf x [1.1, 0.9, 1.2, 0.8]);
np200 (fine grid; single-fault histories H1-H5 only, lines of the map).

## Metrics (reported, no pass/fail gate in v0)
Final-call accuracy per history/truth/excitation/estimator over 5 seeds; calibration = fraction
of minutes after t = 20 where the true (a_c, a_m) lies inside the 95% ellipse; time to a correct
call that then holds to t = 60. Failures are kept and listed.

## Amendment M6-A2 (2026-09-30 03:47 IST, AFTER seeing frozen v0 results; post-hoc, labelled)
