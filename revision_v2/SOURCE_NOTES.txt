1. Simulation-calibrated tests with nuisance parameters

[Dufour2006] Jean-Marie Dufour. Monte Carlo tests with nuisance parameters: A general approach to finite-sample inference and nonstandard asymptotics. Journal of Econometrics 133 (2006), 443–477.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2005.06.007
Primary author version: https://jeanmariedufour.github.io/Dufour_1995_MCT_W.pdf
Local: sources/dufour_mc.pdf; author manuscript dated 2005, distinct from the final journal pagination.
Locations: PDF p.11 (printed p.6), Proposition 2.2 and eq.2.16–2.19; PDF pp.18–20 (printed pp.13–15), section 4, Proposition 4.1/4.2, eq.4.17/4.21. Section 2.2 treats ties.
PARAPHRASED: Exchangeability of the observed and simulated statistics supports Monte Carlo rank tests. Maximizing the simulated p-value over a null nuisance set containing the true nuisance value supplies conservative type-I error control under the stated assumptions. Exact validity here permits conservatism.
DESIGN INFERENCE: Fit bias/severity using the same procedure in observed and simulated replicates. A plug-in nuisance value, a finite nuisance grid, or an empirical quantile from selected training conditions does not automatically establish uniform validity over continuous nuisance parameters. Freeze the model family and tie rule. A sequential stop/extend/act procedure needs calibration of that complete procedure, or separately justified repeated-testing control; a fixed-test result alone does not establish stopping validity.
Limit: No claim that the proposed SSMR score already satisfies these assumptions. Empirical held-out validation remains necessary and is scoped to its declared data-generating conditions.

[Morris2019] Tim P. Morris, Ian R. White, Michael J. Crowther. Using simulation studies to evaluate statistical methods. Statistics in Medicine 38(11) (2019), 2074–2102.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086
Primary repository: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10066118/
Primary PDF: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/10066118/1/2019%20-%20Morris%20-%20simulation%20studies%20tutorial%20-%20stat%20med.pdf
Local: sources/morris_simulation.pdf.
Locations: PDF p.4, section 3 (ADEMP); PDF p.13, Table 6; section 5.2 and simulation-reporting discussion.
PARAPHRASED: Plan aims, data-generating mechanisms, estimands, methods and performance measures explicitly; report Monte Carlo uncertainty in estimated performance. Coverage and rejection rates have sampling uncertainty from finite independent repetitions.
DESIGN INFERENCE: Reserve separate calibration/design-selection and evaluation seeds; report true-cause rejection, wrong unique diagnosis, correct unique diagnosis, inconclusive outcomes and unconditional completion alongside conditional timing. Use whole experimental episodes as the independent units when samples within an episode are correlated. A held-out simulation result supports the evaluated mechanisms, not plant-wide validity or physical validation.
Limit: This source is guidance for evaluating methods, not a proof of validity of the proposed diagnostic score.

2. Active diagnosis and membrane-reformer context

[Scott2014] Joseph K. Scott, Rolf Findeisen, Richard D. Braatz, Davide M. Raimondo. Input design for guaranteed fault diagnosis using zonotopes. Automatica 50 (2014), 1580–1589.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2014.03.016
Primary author PDF: https://web.mit.edu/braatzgroup/input_design_for_guaranteed_fault_diagnosis_using_zonotopes.pdf
Local: sources/scott_active.pdf.
Locations: PDF p.1 abstract/introduction; section 2 problem formulation.
PARAPHRASED: This work designs separating inputs for fault modes represented by linear systems with bounded uncertainty while accounting for disturbance to other control objectives.
DESIGN INFERENCE: Active excitation for diagnosis is established prior methodology. The current paper's contribution should be a scoped nonlinear benchmark result and verified decision consequences, rather than novelty of the general idea of making a feed move.
Limit: Bounded linear-model guarantees do not transfer directly to a nonlinear SSMR with Gaussian errors, nuisance bias and evolving deterioration. A fixed tested feed step is not evidence of global minimum disruption.

Primary author PDF: https://web.mit.edu/braatzgroup/Raimondo_Automatica_2016.pdf
Local: sources/raimondo_closed.pdf.
Location: PDF p.1 abstract/introduction and stated model assumptions.
PARAPHRASED: The method extends separating-input design to polytopic uncertainty and combines it with a constrained-zonotope observer in a moving-horizon closed-loop scheme. Online information can change the diagnostic input decision.

3. Instrument uncertainty: what can and cannot be sourced

[JCGM100] Joint Committee for Guides in Metrology. Evaluation of measurement data — Guide to the expression of uncertainty in measurement. JCGM 100:2008.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E
Official guide index: https://www.bipm.org/en/web/guest/publications/guides
Official PDF: https://www.bipm.org/documents/20126/2071204/JCGM_100_2008_E.pdf/cb0ef43f-baa5-11cf-3f85-4dcd86f77bd6
Local: sources/jcgm_100.pdf.
Locations: clauses 4.3.3–4.3.7 (printed pp.12–13); F.2.3.1–F.2.4.2 (printed p.65, PDF p.77).
PARAPHRASED: An uncertainty statement may represent a standard deviation, a multiple thereof, an interval or only bounds. Conversion requires knowing what it means and how it was obtained. A distribution assigned to bounds is an assumption requiring justification; it is not automatically Gaussian repeatability noise.
DESIGN INFERENCE: Keep random repeatability, calibration bias, drift, gain error and response time separate. Until the actual instrument/configuration and relevant calibration or repeatability data are available, the diagnostic noise inputs must remain declared simulation assumptions/sensitivity cases. A manufacturer accuracy specification alone does not establish independent, per-sample Gaussian standard deviations.

Optional manufacturer identity corroboration (not a diagnostic-noise source): Agilent Instrument & Accessories Catalog, 2009–2010 edition, printed pp.21–22, identifies the 3000A MicroGC family and configurations.
https://www.agilent.com/cs/library/catalogs/Public/5990-3699EN.pdf
Only the relevant catalogue entry was inspected online; not included among the locally downloaded PDFs. The actual rig's full configuration and repeatability under the SSMR gas mixture remain unresolved. No catalogue accuracy/RSD value was adopted.

Use in this revision
The substantive repair remains a frozen, observation-based diagnostic procedure with justified covariance/nuisance treatment, held-out episode-level evaluation of the complete acquisition/decision sequence, and implementable actions evaluated under the actual true fault. Reordering the headline, disclosing the threshold chronology and adding instrument specifications improve clarity, but cannot substitute for those checks. These are design recommendations grounded in the original review gaps and the bounded source claims above, not new evaluated findings from this source lane.
