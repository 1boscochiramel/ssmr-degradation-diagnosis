Evidence boundaries
drop denotes the nominal quasi-steady map H2 loss at t10. Metadata reports the
actual dynamic true and biased-observed loss separately. Core starts are settled
at fixed scheduled t0 health with recorded convergence gates; deterioration then
continues within the ODE. Commands change at 10 minutes, decisions use states at
16 or 21 minutes, and physical costs use only the causal prefix plus the chosen
branch through 31 minutes.
Feed integrals respect command switches; actual feed/H2 use the declared 0.1 min
trapezoidal quadrature. Sensor assumptions are synthetic and conditional on the
declared instrument model; no measured-device accuracy or financial payback is
established. Unsupported/excluded scenarios and failures must remain visible.
Active-minus-passive outcomes describe the whole feed-and-measurement sequence.
The feed pulse itself changes production; without a matched feed-only comparator,
these differences are not an information-specific value-of-information estimate.
