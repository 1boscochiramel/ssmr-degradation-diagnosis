PRE-EVALUATION POLICY AMENDMENT - 2026-09-30

The published demand is rounded. The original frozen command-selection helper
compared nominal healthy predictions to this rounded value; even a zero-fault
prediction could consequently select the next coarse feed grid point. This was
identified by source/analytic review before any stochastic calibration or
evaluation. It was not selected after observing policy-performance outcomes.

The revised policy uses the nominal healthy map output at baseline command as
the restoring target for every candidate. A64-machine-epsilon relative allowance
addresses floating-point evaluation roundoff only. Physical outcomes continue
to use the benchmark's declared demand, and the difference is disclosed.

The historical revision_model.py and protocol.json remain unchanged with their
original design hashes. revision_policy.py is the explicitly selected replacement
command policy. Diagnostic scores, covariance, template bank, stochastic law,
calibration method, truth cases, action grid and physical cost integration are
unchanged. No calibration or evaluation data existed at this amendment.

Required independent analytic check: every zero-fault candidate in either mode
selects baseline command at both decision times; different physical command
choices must retain their actual integrated costs even when action labels agree.
