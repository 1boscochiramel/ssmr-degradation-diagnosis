INDEPENDENT CHECKER DESIGN - PROVISIONAL, NOT FROZEN
Owner cx-check. Root must confirm implementation/schema before freeze.
This is same-model internal verification, not cross-model or physical validation.

Purpose
Repair the specific statistical and accounting defects identified in the external
review. A scientifically unsuccessful evaluation can pass the integrity checks.
Do not tune thresholds, feature sets, candidate limits, seeds or stopping rules
after viewing held-out evaluation. Preserve earlier frozen/A1/A2 evidence.

Recommended statistical construction
1. Freeze the candidate family, dynamic path parameterization, nuisance bias
   treatment, all sensor acquisition rules, excitation, allowed decision looks,
   score, fit grid/optimizer, noise law and the truth/noise strata before data.
   Include healthy behavior or explicitly delimit its out-of-family status.
2. Fit each candidate to actual simulated observations available at that look.
   Never insert zero for the known generating label. The diagnostic function
   must not receive true hypothesis, latent health, latent bias or future data.
3. Use a joint score retaining shared-baseline covariance. For disjoint raw
   mean errors e1,e2 with variances v1,v2 and a constant per-run bias b,
   [mean1, mean2-mean1] has covariance [[v1,-v1],[-v1,v1+v2]] conditional on b.
   If b is integrated as an independent random effect, add Var(b) to the first
   diagonal only. A random bias is not fresh independent noise at every sample.
   More generally use an explicit aggregation matrix A and raw covariance K:
   summary covariance = A K A^T. Retain overlap, sensor correlation and missing
   delayed samples. Constant bias must appear once per run/sensor or as a
   shared nuisance parameter, never average away by increasing sample count.
4. Calibrate the complete sequential event. For each independent calibration
   trajectory take the maximum true-candidate score across all allowed looks
   on the prespecified acquisition path. Use one rank threshold per candidate
   and calibration stratum: k=ceil((n+1)*(1-alpha)), threshold=ordered score k.
   If k>n use +infinity. Reject only for score>threshold (ties retained).
   The same score/fitting/acquisition procedure must apply in calibration/eval.
5. A maximum of per-stratum thresholds gives marginal protection for each
   included finite stratum. Pooling fixed-grid strata instead supports only a
   declared sampled mixture; do not claim coverage uniform over continuous
   severities, unseen dynamics, arbitrary bias or model mismatch. A candidate
   surviving every look may be retained by intersecting sets over looks.
   Singleton wrong calls imply true-label exclusion only when truth is in the
   covered candidate family. Empty set is MODEL_INCOMPATIBLE, not a
   confident nearest-label diagnosis. Multiple survivors are INCONCLUSIVE.
6. The rank argument treats whole independent trajectories as exchangeable
   units, not the serial samples inside one trajectory. Adaptive acquisition
   must match the calibrated policy; precomputing later unused data cannot
   justify using it in an earlier decision. A fixed maximum over all scheduled
   diagnostic looks is simplest; physical action may begin only after the
   selected stopping time and is audited separately.

Evaluation and uncertainty
Use explicit disjoint seed namespaces for design/debug, calibration, evaluation,
and mismatch sensitivity. Reusing deterministic noiseless plant trajectories
across noise realizations is allowed but limits claims to those plant strata.
Independent noise seeds alone do not constitute new independent physical plants.
Report per-stratum and declared-mixture denominators, true-label ever-excluded,
wrong singleton call, correct singleton call, inconclusive and empty-set rates.
All trajectories remain in denominators, including failures. Report decision
time distributions with no-call censoring and conditional denominators; do not
present successful-only median as unconditional delay. Report onset-adjusted
delay separately from simulation clock time.
For independent evaluation seeds within a fixed stratum, provide exact binomial
confidence intervals from the raw event counts. Correlated time points are not
additional trials. Pooled heterogeneous deterministic-stratum counts must not
be labelled exact binomial without iid mixture sampling. No empirical target is
forced: report empirical rates and intervals. A claim of rate<=alpha requires
its prespecified one-sided confidence upper bound<=alpha; lower>alpha contradicts
that claim; otherwise evidence is insufficient for that empirical assertion.
The marginal calibration statement is distinct from fixed-calibration empirical
performance and needs its exchangeability/model assumptions stated.

Physical actions and losses
Actions are feed commands or feedback policies, with timestamps and actuator
bounds, not names. For every truth trajectory and chosen alternative action,
save actual feed and hydrogen production over the same fixed horizon. Integrate
H2 shortfall=max(demand-production,0), total actual ethanol, and command ethanol
separately if a feed-delivery fault exists. Compute differences against explicit
paired baselines/oracle policies using the same truth/noise scenario and horizon.
Include the excitation interval in total feed and hydrogen accounts. Never set
loss differences to zero merely because both labels say COMPENSATE. Preserve
signed net feed differences; distinguish excess feed from total feed. Service
cost/downtime are unavailable: report service requests, not financial costs or
payback. No optimally weighted VOI claim without an explicit prior/loss rule.
If action outcomes use surrogate or lookup responses, label that scope and save
the map/interpolation provenance; do not call them full dynamic plant reruns.

Proposed raw interface (final file names can be agreed with implementation)
protocol.json: revision ID, exact scenario/candidate/parameter grid, sensors,
units, scheduled sample/arrival/decision times, covariance model, fitting rule,
alpha, calibration strata, seed counts/namespaces, actions, quadrature, horizon,
all source/input hashes. No unknown fields should control evaluation behavior.
seed_manifest.csv: split, stratum_id, run_id, seed, RNG algorithm/version; no
calibration/evaluation seed overlap. Shared sensor streams between compared
policies must have explicit pairing IDs, never count as independent replication.
observations.csv: run_id, sensor, sample_id, sample_time, arrival_time,
truth_value, bias_component, noise_component, measured_value, used_phase.
truth fields are for audit only and excluded from diagnostic function inputs.
looks.jsonl: run_id, look_id, decision_time, consumed_sample_ids, aggregation
matrix/order, observed summary, covariance, candidate fit parameters and
predicted summary, residual, score, threshold, survivors, decision/reason.
Full fit-grid objective vectors or a saved bounded search trace permit independent
minimum checks; fitting status/bound contacts must be retained, never discarded.
calibration.csv: candidate, stratum, seed/run_id, each true-candidate look score,
maximum score. thresholds.json: n, alpha, rank, threshold and input hashes.
physical_trace.csv: run_id, policy, interval start/end, demand, H2 production,
command ethanol, actual ethanol, true evolving health/fault, phase, action ID.
Use explicit interval-rate convention or endpoint samples plus fixed quadrature.
outcomes.csv: run_id, truth, final outcome, stop/onset times, event flags, physical
integrals and paired-reference IDs. These are recomputed, not trusted inputs.
execution.json/source_inventory.json: start/end, exits/errors, input/source
hashes, calibration/evaluation completion, file hashes and package versions.

Checker acceptance
Integrity PASS requires frozen hashes, source isolation, complete declared
scenarios and seeds, no forbidden split leakage, finite raw records, causality,
sampling/arrival correctness, true paths consistent with declared faults,
independent covariance/score/threshold/decision reconstruction, physical-integral
reconstruction and exact headline/denominator agreement. Numerical tolerances
are frozen from declared output precision and solver settings, not chosen from
observed discrepancies. Scientific performance is a separate result, not an
integrity gate. A missing raw intermediate is UNCHECKABLE, not PASS.
Negative controls before evaluation: seed overlap, true-label zero insertion,
future GC use, wrong-sign covariance, threshold rank off-by-one, same-action
zero cost, wrong quadrature, dropped inconclusive run and changed headline.

Statistical source basis (read 2026-09-30)
Lei et al., Distribution-Free Predictive Inference for Regression, section 2:
https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~ryantibs/papers/conformal-jasa.pdf
The finite-sample rank statement applies to exchangeable scalar scores. The
trajectory-maximum and finite-stratum construction above is this reviewer's
application of that rank argument, not an assertion made about this benchmark
by the cited authors. No guarantee is claimed under distribution shift.
NIST exact binomial confidence limits:
https://itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbici.htm
SciPy exact interval implementation:
https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.binomtest.html

Implementation agreement update (before evaluation)
Root accepts per-stratum maximum-over-looks rank thresholds, then maximum over
covered finite strata per hypothesis/configuration; alpha=.05, calibration499,
evaluation1000 per declared deterministic history. These are design inputs,
not observed success counts. Covered truths and off-grid/model-mismatch stress
sets are separate. Candidate sets use cumulative intersection and early
singleton at16min; otherwise final look21min yields singleton, INCONCLUSIVE or
MODEL_INCOMPATIBLE. A label confidence set is not a parameter confidence region.
The fixed bank includes zero-fault paths under several labels, overlapping
with H; those cases can legitimately remain inconclusive.
Statistics use absolute disjoint-window means with full same-sensor AR1 and
persistent uniform-bias covariance. Windows (0,5], (5,10], (11,16], and at21
also (16,21]; every available historical GC sample is assigned by acquisition
time, with arrival cutoff at each look. GC every3min, delay3min; fast every.1min.
Correlation uses sample-order lag within each sensor, not arrival order.
Noise/bias/phi stresses that keep nominal covariance are explicitly scored
with a nominal working covariance. Separate calibration or stress labelling is
required; true covariance equality cannot be claimed for altered noise laws.
Physical commanded feed integrates constant command per interval exactly;
actual feed under F uses that interval command times evolving F at endpoints.
H2 and actual feed quadrature are endpoint trapezoids within each interval,
without smearing a command switch across old and new commands. The active pulse
ends when diagnosis stops; prefixes and branch continuations share exact states.
Initial state protocol is being amended before freeze to fixed-health
preconditioning from source IC, with serialized convergence gates and trace.
Checker must verify source IC -> preconditioned state -> dynamic state0 ->
decision state -> action branch chain. Historical healthy-IC initialization is
a separate explicit stress. Mode2F nominal-map positive drops are unsupported:
require explicit reachability mask/reasons instead of silent denominator loss.
All diagnostic batch summaries/scores/fit IDs are saved. Full raw observation
regeneration is acceptable from frozen RNG algorithm/version, batch seed,
within-batch index, settings and noiseless truth-frame hash, with raw exemplars
saved for cross-check; this is an explicitly checkable provenance mechanism.

Recorded amendments and final evidence interpretation
The original model/protocol and numerical acceptance functions remain frozen.
The separate policy amendment uses the exact healthy map value at baseline
command as its restoration target. Its zero-fault baseline-command checks were
completed and frozen before any stochastic evaluation.

The first complete dynamic audit exposed a checker parent-lookup defect: healthy
and sensor-bias-only scenarios can have identical physical state hashes. A
versioned correction includes scenario identity in the lookup key. It changes
no numerical or exact-state requirement. A further read-only adapter maps the
recorded source root to an explicitly supplied artifact root for source hashing
and initial-condition reading; no recorded metadata is rewritten.

The original exact exported-first-state gate remains failed. The separately
frozen forensic replay tests a different statement: solver initial arrays equal
the intended input arrays exactly, and the full original exported state arrays,
truth CSV bytes and interval CSV bytes reproduce exactly. The discrepancy arises
in dense-output evaluation at a segment's initial time. This is a post hoc
provenance resolution, not a numerical tolerance change or a newly passed
original gate. Original failure output is preserved in dynamic_verification.json.
The final wrapper may accept explicitly qualified computational verification
only when every remaining discrepancy is this identified export condition,
every affected record has exact forensic replay evidence, and all original
sources/results remain unchanged. It exposes original_frozen_dynamic_gate_pass
as false and binds the forensic evidence and corrected checker separately.

run_revision_checks.py is the final integration/report-binding entry point.
Arguments --root, --prior-audit-root and --original-archive-path select the
revision tree, extracted historical audit tree and original supplied ZIP.
The final wrapper audits this recorded campaign including its preserved failure
and forensic evidence. Fresh simulations must create their own raw checks and
provenance evidence; copied old evidence cannot establish a fresh run's result.
All verification remains internal and same-model, with no independent reactor
